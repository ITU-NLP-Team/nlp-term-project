\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \sys, a hybrid retrieval and reranking system for RETECO Track~1, and used it to
measure, on the complete data, which ideas transfer to temporal reasoning-intensive retrieval.
Three findings stand out. (1)~Retrieval granularity matters more than temporal machinery:
passage-level BM25 alone improves the official baseline by more than 70\%. (2)~Explicit dates
are not a discriminative relevance signal on TEMPO---gold evidence mentions the query's years
less often than the documents BM25 already ranks highly---so year priors, year filters and a
year--entity graph do not help---nor do periods inferred by an LLM for every document and
question---while semantic retrieval does: a 0.6B instruction-tuned
embedder is the strongest single channel, and a temporal RAPTOR tree matches but does not
exceed it. \ConclusionLLM{} (3)~\ConclusionRerank{}

The final system reaches \rFinalA{} (1a) and \rFinalB{} (1b) nDCG@10 on dev, \gainOfficialA\%
and \gainOfficialB\% above the official baseline, and \pooledFinalA{} on all TEMPO questions,
above every retriever on the TEMPO leaderboard. Promising next steps are stronger reasoning
for the reasoning-trained retriever (the largest gap to the leaderboard's best setting),
rerankers fine-tuned on TEMPO's train judgments, the 4B reranker for steps, and LLM-written
summaries at the upper levels of the temporal tree.
