\begin{abstract}
Temporal reasoning-intensive retrieval asks for evidence that is not only on topic but useful
for the period and events a question depends on. We describe \sys, our system for both
sub-tracks of RETECO Track~1 (SemEval-2027 Task~1), evaluated on all 13 domains and 1.17M
documents. \sys retrieves 200-word passages with BM25, a dense embedder and a reasoning-trained
retriever (ReasonIR-8B), expands each question with an LLM---a hypothetical answer, sub-queries,
the events \emph{neighbouring} the question, and free-text reasoning---fuses the channels chosen
on train, and reranks the top-100 with a decoder LLM. Matching dates, by contrast, does not
help, even when periods are inferred by an LLM for every document and question: gold documents
mention the question's years \emph{less} often than the non-relevant documents BM25 ranks
highly. With every choice made on train, \sys reaches \rFinalA{} (1a) and \rFinalB{} (1b)
nDCG@10 on dev, \gainOfficialA\% and \gainOfficialB\% above the official baseline. On all
1{,}730 TEMPO questions it scores \pooledFinalA{} (nDCG@10$\times$100), above the best retriever
on the TEMPO leaderboard (32.0).
\end{abstract}
