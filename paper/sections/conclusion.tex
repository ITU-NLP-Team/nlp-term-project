\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sys shows four things on the complete RETECO Track~1 data. (1)~Retrieval granularity matters
more than temporal machinery: passage BM25 alone improves the official baseline by over 70\%.
(2)~Explicit dates are not a useful relevance signal: gold evidence mentions the question's years
less often than what BM25 already ranks highly, so year filters, priors and graphs do not help,
whether dates come from rules or an LLM. (3)~Semantics helps: a reasoning-trained retriever is
the strongest single channel, and LLM expansions, above all neighbouring events, add complementary
candidates. (4)~Decoder LLM rerankers clearly beat encoder cross-encoders. The largest remaining
gap is the quality of the reasoning given to the reasoning-trained retriever; stronger reasoning,
a reranker fine-tuned on TEMPO's train judgments and the 4B reranker for steps are next.
