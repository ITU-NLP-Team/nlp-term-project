\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Data and protocol.} We use the latest RETECO release (v1.1, deduplicated Track~1
corpora) for all 13 domains, without sub-sampling: 1{,}211 train and 519 dev queries (1a) and
2{,}762 train and 1{,}214 dev steps (1b) over 1.17M documents (2.41M passages). Every choice
that is not fixed a priori---RM3~$\alpha$, prior weight~$\lambda$, the pool's channels, pure
vs.\ fused reranking, the final configuration---is selected on \textbf{train}; dev is only
evaluated, and pool channels and the final reranker are selected separately for 1a and 1b.
The 4B cross-decoder was run on train and dev 1a and on 12 of the 13 dev domains of 1b; within
our GPU allocation it could not also be run on the 2{,}762 train steps and the 636 dev steps of
\emph{history}, so it is a candidate for 1a only, and its 1b column is left empty. Guidance is used only to build the planner's demonstration (train) and for
post-hoc analysis (dev). Scoring follows the official protocol: \texttt{pytrec\_eval}
\texttt{ndcg\_cut\_10} \citep{vangysel2018pytreceval} per topic, topics missing from a run
count as zero, mean per domain, macro mean over domains; we also report recall@100.
Significance is a two-sided paired randomisation test over topics pooled across domains
(10{,}000 permutations, \citealp{smucker2007significance}).

\paragraph{Baselines.} The official baseline is document-level Lucene BM25 (Pyserini analyser,
$k_1{=}0.9$, $b{=}0.4$). Our first row uses the same BM25 parameters on passages with MaxP.

\paragraph{Models.} Dense: Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B (fp16, 384 tokens). Cross-encoders:
\texttt{ms-marco-MiniLM-L12-v2} (512 tokens) and \texttt{bge-reranker-v2-m3} (1{,}024).
Cross-decoders: Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B and -4B (bf16, 1{,}024). Generator: Qwen3-8B (bf16,
non-thinking, greedy decoding) served offline with vLLM~0.8.5. All models are open-weight; no
model is fine-tuned on RETECO.

\paragraph{Compute.} Indexing and all GPU stages ran on the UHeM Altay cluster (single NVIDIA
A100 80GB per job, up to four jobs in parallel). \computenote{} The pipeline also runs end to
end on a CPU-only laptop (8~GB RAM) with BM25, \texttt{bge-small}, MiniLM and a 1.5B GGUF
generator, which we used for development.
