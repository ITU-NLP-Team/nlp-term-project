\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Data and protocol.} We use RETECO v1.1 for all 13 domains, without sub-sampling:
1{,}211 train and 519 dev questions (1a), 2{,}762 train and 1{,}214 dev steps (1b). Every choice
not fixed a priori (channels, weights, reranker, ablation parameters) is made on \textbf{train};
dev is only evaluated. Guidance is used only for the expansion demonstration (train) and for
analysis. Scoring follows the official protocol (\texttt{pytrec\_eval} \texttt{ndcg\_cut\_10},
\citealp{vangysel2018pytreceval}; missing topics count as zero; macro mean over domains).
Significance is a paired randomisation test over topics pooled across domains (10{,}000
permutations, \citealp{smucker2007significance}).

\paragraph{Models and compute.} All models are open-weight and none is fine-tuned on RETECO
(Appendix~\ref{app:hparams}). GPU work ran on the UHeM Altay cluster at Istanbul Technical
University, on nodes with four NVIDIA A100 80\,GB GPUs; the most expensive stage, embedding
2.41M passages with ReasonIR-8B, took 20 A100-hours (Table~\ref{tab:compute}). The 4B reranker
was not run on the 2{,}762 train steps of 1b, so it is a candidate for 1a only.
