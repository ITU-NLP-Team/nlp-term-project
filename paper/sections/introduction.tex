\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Retrieval-augmented generation assumes that the retriever surfaces evidence that is valid
\emph{for the question being asked}. For temporally grounded questions---``Why did the number
of transactions per block jump in 2023?''---validity depends on time: a passage on the right
topic but about the wrong period is misleading evidence. Track~1 of the RETECO shared task
(SemEval-2027 Task~1) operationalises this with TEMPO \citep{abdallah2026tempo}, a 13-domain
benchmark of StackExchange questions whose relevant documents must be both topical and
temporally useful. The official BM25 baseline reaches only \rOfficialA{} (Sub-track~1a) and
\rOfficialB{} (1b) nDCG@10 on the development set.

A natural reading of the task is that systems should \emph{match time}: extract dates from
questions and documents and restrict or boost by period. We tested this directly, with
rule-based years and with periods that a small LLM infers for every document and question, and
found that it does not help. Among questions that mention a year, only \pctGoldOverlap{} of gold
documents mention a year within $\pm1$ of it, against \pctTopOverlap{} of the non-relevant
documents that BM25 ranks in its top~10 (Table~\ref{tab:data}): lexical retrieval already
over-rewards matching dates. Relevant evidence instead describes the events and mechanisms
behind a question, often without repeating its dates.

\sys is built on that observation. It retrieves passages rather than whole documents, which
alone lifts BM25 from \rOfficialA{} to \rBmA{} (1a); it adds a dense embedder and a
reasoning-trained retriever (ReasonIR-8B); and it lets an LLM expand each question with a
hypothetical answer, sub-queries, the events \emph{neighbouring} the question (its causes,
earlier and later events, each with a year) and free-text reasoning. The channels to fuse are
selected on train, and a decoder LLM reranks the top-100. On dev, \sys reaches \rFinalA{}
nDCG@10 on 1a and \rFinalB{} on 1b, \gainOfficialA\% and \gainOfficialB\% above the official
baseline. Because RETECO's train and dev questions are the 1{,}730 TEMPO questions, we can also
place it on the TEMPO leaderboard (Table~\ref{tab:tempo}): with \pooledFinalA{} on questions it
is above all twelve published retrievers, though below a reasoning retriever given GPT-4o's
reasoning (41.0).

Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item A system for both sub-tracks that combines passage retrieval, dense and
        reasoning-trained retrieval, LLM query expansion with neighbouring events, a
        train-selected fusion and LLM reranking, evaluated on all 13 domains without
        sub-sampling.
  \item Evidence that date matching is not a useful relevance signal on TEMPO, whether dates
        come from rules or from an LLM.
  \item A comparison of encoder cross-encoders and decoder LLM rerankers on the same
        candidates, and a comparison with the TEMPO leaderboard on the same questions.
  \item Open code that runs on a GPU cluster and on a laptop CPU, with cached stages, official
        scoring and significance tests.\footnote{Code:
        \url{https://github.com/ITU-NLP-Team/nlp-term-project}}
\end{itemize}
